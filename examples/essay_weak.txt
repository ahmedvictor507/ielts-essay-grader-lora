Nowadays many people go to university. Some people think university must teach practical skill for get job, and other people think academic knowledge are more important. I will discuss both view in this essay.

Firstly, practical skill is very important because student need job after university. If student learn only theory he cannot work good in company. For example my friend study engineering but he not know how use machine, so company not take him. Also practical skill make student have more confident.

In the other hand, academic knowledge is important too. Without knowledge student cannot understand the thing. Teacher say that knowledge is base of everything. Also some jobs like doctor need many knowledge, so university must teach it.

In my opinion both are good but I think practical is more good because life is hard and people need money. If university teach practical skill, student can get job fast and help family. So university should focus on this more than academic things.

In conclusion, there are two view about this topic. Practical skill is important and academic knowledge is important too, but I prefer practical skill because it help student to find job.
